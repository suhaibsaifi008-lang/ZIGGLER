# Canva

Learned: 2026-08-24 00:39

- 1. Sign up or log in to Canva. — Access your Canva account.
- 2. Select a template. — Choose a pre-designed template from Canva's library.
- 3. Edit the template. — Drag and drop elements, add text, and adjust layout.
- 4. Save your design. — Click 'Save' to keep your work.
- 5. Export your design. — Choose the desired format and download your design.

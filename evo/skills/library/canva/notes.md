# Canva

Learned: 2026-08-24 01:45

- 1. Log in to Canva — Open Canva.com and enter your credentials.
- 2. Select a template — Navigate to the homepage and choose a template that suits your needs.
- 3. Customize the template — Drag and drop elements, change colors, and add text to personalize your design.
- 4. Save or export your design — Click on the 'Save' icon and choose how to export your design (PDF, PNG, JPEG, etc.).
- 5. Publish your design — Optionally, share your design directly from Canva or export it to other platforms like email, social media, or Google Drive.
